Project 1: Moving Car Detection and Tracking

Detect moving cars in video and track their direction. This project also counts vehicles moving toward the city center.

Report

The report explains two motion-detection approaches: frame differencing and background subtraction. Both methods compare pixel values between frames to isolate moving objects while suppressing static background content.

  • Task 1 pipeline: convert each frame to grayscale, apply blur for noise reduction, use a background frame, compute frame difference, and detect contours for moving cars.
  • Task 2 extends the method with a region of interest to count cars moving toward downtown on a two-way road.
  • The counting logic includes a cooldown window across frames so one car is not counted multiple times and short detection glitches are ignored.
  • Final outputs include detected car counts and normalized rate metrics based on video duration.

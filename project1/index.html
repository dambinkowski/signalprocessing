Moving Car Detection and Tracking

Motion detection in traffic video using frame differencing and background subtraction, with directional counting toward the city center.

Computer Vision Motion Detection Vehicle Counting

Report Summary

The pipeline compares frame-level pixel differences to isolate moving cars while suppressing background noise and static scene content.

  • Converts frames to grayscale and blurs before differencing for stable contour extraction.
  • Introduces a focused region of interest for directional counting on a two-way road.
  • Uses cooldown logic to avoid multiple counts of the same vehicle across nearby frames.
  • Produces final car totals and normalized traffic rates over the video duration.
